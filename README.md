# 🎨 Creative Royalties Everywhere – Blockchain Based Marketplace for UI/UX Creators and Digital Artists 
*Empowering digital designers with true ownership, royalties, and recognition.*

---

## 🚀 Overview

**Creative Royalties Everywhere** is a decentralized marketplace built on **Algorand** that allows UI/UX designers and digital artists to **mint, sell, and license** their creative work as **NFT-backed collectibles**.

Whether it’s a **minimal UI kit**, an **icon pack**, or a **motion design**, creators can tokenize their work, enforce royalties on resales, and gain global visibility — all with full ownership and on-chain verification.

---

## 🧩 Key Features

- ✅ **NFT Minting for Creators**  
  Designers can mint UI kits, icon sets, wireframes, and other digital assets as NFTs using decentralized metadata.

- 💸 **Automated Royalty Distribution**  
  Built-in smart contracts ensure artists earn royalties every time their work is resold based on their licensing terms.

- 🔁 **Transparent Resale Marketplace**  
  Buyers can purchase and resell assets with full on-chain proof of ownership and built-in creator rights.
